# 5-Pillar Human Experience Scale

> *"A framework for mapping consciousness, existential drives, and personal fulfillment across multidimensional cognitive axes."*

---

## 🧭 Introduction

The **5-Pillar Human Experience Scale** is an existential and psychological framework designed to model human consciousness not as a rigid checklist, but as a dynamic, multidimensional space. 

Traditional models of psychological "flourishing" often assume a universal recipe—implying that a meaningful life requires high levels of emotional depth, philosophical abstraction, and existential urgency. This scale rejects that uniformity. Instead, it posits that individuals operate with vastly different baseline "intake requirements" (or cognitive overhead) across five core dimensions of the human condition.

---

## 🏛️ The Five Pillars

Every human mind operates across these five fundamental axes:

| Pillar | Definition | Core Question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Curiosity** | The drive to investigate, learn, and comprehend mechanics. | *"How does this work?"* |
| **2. Agency** | The intent and capacity to choose, act, and alter reality. | *"What can I change?"* |
| **3. Mortality** | The awareness of finitude, time constraints, and ultimate stakes. | *"How much time do I have left?"* |
| **4. Empathy** | The capacity and desire to resonate with other subjective minds. | *"What are they feeling?"* |
| **5. Abstraction** | The ability to encode reality into symbols, stories, and meta-concepts. | *"What does this mean?"* |

---

## 💡 Core Philosophical Principles

### 1. Fulfillment Range vs. Current Actual Level
For each pillar, a person has two distinct data points:
* **Fulfillment Range (Min – Max):** The bandwidth in which an individual feels satisfied and balanced. Operating below the minimum causes stagnation or starvation; operating above the maximum causes burnout or overwhelming friction.
* **Current Actual Level:** Where a person’s life realistically sits right now on that axis.

### 2. High-Overhead vs. Low-Overhead Minds
The scale explains why people with vastly different worldviews can both function normally and feel fulfilled:
* **High-Overhead Minds (e.g., Seekers, Humanists):** Run with intense internal motion—high curiosity, deep abstraction, constant self-reflection, and existential friction.
* **Low-Overhead Minds (e.g., Radical Pragmatists, Anchors):** Run on low existential friction—direct input-output loops, concrete operational thinking, and steady routine execution. 

Neither style is "more human" than the other; they are simply different geometric configurations of the same underlying hardware.

## 📊 About the Visualization Tool

Included in this repository is an interactive web application built to visualize these profiles:
* **Master Hero Chart:** An overlapping radar chart allowing you to compare multiple participant profiles simultaneously.
* **Small-Multiples Grid:** Individual participant cards showing their live slider controls, range locks, and mini radar profiles.
* **Group Heatmap Matrix:** A bird's-eye tabular view comparing actual levels across an entire group.
* **Real-Time Room Sync:** Firebase-powered multi-device cloud syncing so friends can join a room and watch each other's graphs update live.
